Data Deletion Instructions

Earth, Briefly · last updated November 19, 2026

The app “claude videos” does not store personal data about Facebook users. Only the Page owner signs in to it.

To remove the app and any associated data

  1. On Facebook, open Settings & privacy → Settings.
  2. Go to Apps and websites.
  3. Find claude videos and click Remove.

This revokes the app’s access immediately. If you want us to confirm that nothing about you is stored, send a message to the Earth, Briefly Facebook Page and we will reply within 30 days.
