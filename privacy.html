Privacy Policy

Earth, Briefly · last updated November 19, 2026

Earth, Briefly is a Facebook Page that shares short videos of landscapes. The app “claude videos” is an internal tool used only by the Page owner to upload and schedule those videos on the Page.

What data we collect

The app does not collect, store or process personal data from people who view or interact with the Page. It does not offer login to the public.

The only account that signs in to the app is the Page owner’s, to obtain a Page access token. That token is stored privately on the owner’s computer and is used only to publish videos to the Earth, Briefly Page.

How we use data

The app only uploads videos and captions to the Page and checks their publishing status. We do not sell, share or use any data for advertising or profiling.

Third parties

Videos are published through the Meta Graph API. Your use of Facebook is governed by Meta’s Privacy Policy. Video footage comes from stock libraries under licenses that allow this use; credits are given in the post when the license requires it.
